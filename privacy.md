# I Am Worthy — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2 May 2026**

I Am Worthy is designed to work on your device. This policy explains, in plain language, what that means for your data.

## Short version

**I Am Worthy does not collect or transmit personal information.** The app has no servers, no user accounts, and no advertising. The only data that leaves your device is a small number of anonymous usage signals sent to TelemetryDeck (described below). Anything you type — your custom affirmations — stays on your device.

## Detailed version

### What the app stores on your device

Any custom affirmations you add are stored locally in an App Group on your iPhone, alongside your selected rotation frequency. If you have a paired Apple Watch with the I Am Worthy watch app installed, those values are mirrored to the watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. None of this data is uploaded to any server we control.

### What the app reads from your device

- **The current date**, provided by iOS, used only to select which affirmation to display for the day. This value never leaves your device.

The app requests no other system permissions — no access to contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera, health data, notifications, or tracking identifiers.

### Anonymized usage analytics

I Am Worthy sends a small number of anonymous usage signals to [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com), a privacy-focused analytics service. These signals tell us things like "the app launched", "a custom affirmation was added", or "the rotation frequency changed" — nothing more. We use them only to understand how the app is used in aggregate and to spot bugs.

We **never** send the text of your custom affirmations or any other content you type. TelemetryDeck does not collect your IP address or advertising identifier; it derives a salted, hashed identifier on-device that cannot be reversed to identify you. See [TelemetryDeck's privacy statement](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/) for details.

### Other third-party services

None. I Am Worthy does not include any other analytics provider, crash reporter, advertising network, or third-party SDK.

### Children's privacy

I Am Worthy collects no personal data and is safe to use at any age. The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no external links to user-generated content. It is not directed at children under 13, but complies with COPPA by virtue of collecting no personal information.

### Your rights

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to access, export, correct, or delete. This policy is provided to comply with Apple App Store requirements (Guideline 5.1.1) and applicable privacy law — including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

### Changes to this policy

If a future version of I Am Worthy introduces any feature that involves additional data collection, this policy will be updated before that feature ships, the "Last updated" date above will change, and the change will be summarised in the App Store release notes.
