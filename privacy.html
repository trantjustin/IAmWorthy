Privacy

Short version: your affirmations stay on your device.

What we store

I Am Worthy stores any custom affirmations you add, along with their order and rotation frequency, locally on your iPhone. The built-in affirmation list ships with the app. Nothing you type is uploaded to any server we control.

What we don't do

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No servers store your affirmations or any personal data.
  • No advertising IDs, no IDFA, no third-party tracking.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or HealthKit access.

Anonymized usage analytics

I Am Worthy sends a small number of anonymous usage signals to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. These signals tell us things like "the app launched" or "a custom affirmation was added, removed, or reordered" — nothing more. We use them only to understand how the app is used in aggregate and to spot bugs.

We never send the text of your affirmations or any personal data. TelemetryDeck does not collect your IP address or advertising identifier; it derives a salted, hashed identifier on-device that cannot be reversed to identify you. See TelemetryDeck's privacy statement for details.

Backups

Your I Am Worthy data is included in your standard iCloud or iTunes/Finder iPhone backups, alongside the rest of your apps. Those backups are managed by Apple, not by us.

Children

I Am Worthy is safe to use at any age. The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no external links to user-generated content. It is not directed at children under 13, but complies with COPPA by virtue of collecting no personal information.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected from you, there is nothing on our side to access, export, correct, or delete. This policy is provided to comply with Apple App Store requirements (Guideline 5.1.1) and applicable privacy law — including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Changes

If this policy changes in any meaningful way, the updated version will appear at this URL, the "Last updated" date below will change, and the change will be summarised in the App Store release notes.
